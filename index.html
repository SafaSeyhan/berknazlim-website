Spaces shaped by material, light and the people who use them.

Berk Nazlım is an Istanbul-based designer and interior architect, and a 2026 graduate of Istanbul Technical University. His work moves between interiors, cultural buildings and hand-made models.

View projects →About
01 — Approach

Good architecture reads its site, respects its city and gives something back to the people who use it.

Trained in the Department of Interior Architecture at ITU's Faculty of Architecture, Berk has worked on construction sites and in design offices since 2023, from a hotel lobby in Maçka to an apartment in Zeytinburnu. Each project starts from one clear idea and carries it down to the material, the furniture and the detail.
