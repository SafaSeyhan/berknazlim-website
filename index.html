01 — Studio

Good architecture reads its site, respects its city and gives something back to the people who use it.

Trained at ITU's Faculty of Architecture in the historic Taşkışla building, Berk works across scales, from a single pavilion to towers, hotels and whole neighbourhoods. Every project combines careful analysis with a deep respect for place, climate and material.
