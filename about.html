About

Berk Nazlım

Architect. Istanbul Technical University, Faculty of Architecture, class of 2026.

Berk NazlımIstanbul

I design buildings that belong to their place and to their time.

I grew up between the Bosphorus and the hills of Istanbul, a city where Byzantine walls, Ottoman domes and glass towers share the same skyline. That layered city taught me that architecture is always a conversation between past and future.

I studied architecture at Istanbul Technical University, one of the oldest technical universities in the world, in the historic Taşkışla building. There I learned to draw by hand, to think in section and to treat structure, climate and context as the raw material of design.

Today my work combines computational tools with craft: parametric studies to find the right form, and careful detailing to make it real. Whether it is a small house or a tower, the goal is the same: architecture that is bold, generous and built to last.

Principles

What guides the work

01

Context first

Every site has a history, a climate and a community. Good architecture starts by listening to all three.

02

Built to last

Seismic safety, durable materials and low-carbon thinking are not extras. They are the foundation.

03

Bold, never loud

Strong ideas, expressed with restraint and precise detail, age far better than fashion.
