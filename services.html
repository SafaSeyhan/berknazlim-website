Services

From first sketch to finished space

A complete design service, or exactly the part you need. Every project is handled personally by Berk.

01

Interior architecture

Homes, cafés and restaurants designed as one idea: space planning, materials, lighting and furniture made for the space.

  • Space planning
  • Material palettes
  • Lighting
  • Custom furniture and joinery
02

Architectural design

Concept and schematic design for cultural, hospitality and leisure buildings, worked out in plan, section and model.

  • Concept design
  • Schematic design
  • Plans and sections
  • Presentation drawings
03

Visualisation & models

Renders and physical models that let clients see and feel a space before it is built.

  • 3ds Max + Corona renders
  • Lumion and SketchUp
  • Physical models
  • Hand sketches
04

Site & construction

Renovation and repair work followed on site, coordinating builders, schedules and finishes.

  • Site management
  • Renovation
  • Contractor coordination
  • Quality checks
05

Materials & details

Advice on materials, paving and finishes, with detail drawings that solve problems on site.

  • Material selection
  • Stone and paving
  • Detail drawings
  • Investor-side design review
Process

How we work together

Clear stages, clear deliverables and one point of contact from first sketch to handover.

01

Listen

Site visit, brief, budget and ambitions. We agree on what success looks like.

02

Explore

Sketches, models and analysis to test several directions quickly.

03

Refine

One direction developed in detail: plans, materials, structure and cost.

04

Build

Drawings, material choices and close follow-up on site until handover.
