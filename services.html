Services

From first sketch to finished building

A full architectural service, or exactly the part you need. Every project is led personally by Berk.

01

Architectural design

Concept, schematic and developed design through to permit and construction documents for new buildings of every scale.

  • Concept design
  • Permit drawings
  • Construction documents
  • Site supervision
02

Interior architecture

Interiors that carry the building's idea inside: space planning, materials, lighting and bespoke furniture.

  • Space planning
  • Material palettes
  • Lighting
  • Custom joinery
03

Urban design & masterplanning

Neighbourhood plans, public spaces and resilient housing strategies that put people and climate first.

  • Masterplans
  • Public space
  • Feasibility studies
  • Seismic resilience
04

Restoration & adaptive reuse

Surveys, restoration and new uses for existing and historic buildings, keeping their character and their embodied carbon.

  • Building surveys
  • Restoration
  • Change of use
  • Retrofit

Visualisation & models

  • Physical models
  • Walkthroughs

Process

How we work together

Clear stages, clear deliverables and one point of contact from first sketch to handover.

01

Listen

Site visit, brief, budget and ambitions. We agree on what success looks like.

02

Explore

Sketches, models and analysis to test several directions quickly.

03

Refine

One direction developed in detail: plans, materials, structure and cost.

Build

Permits, construction documents and close supervision on site until handover.
